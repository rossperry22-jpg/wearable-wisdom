Does $49 include all four sample designs?

No. The examples show possible layout directions. Your order includes one custom square flyer for one business and one service or offer, plus three captions for that same promotion and one consolidated revision.

What photos and details should I send?

Send your exact business name, service area, chosen service, logo/colors, booking link or phone number, and photos you own or are authorized to use. Include any advertised price, exclusions or conditions. If you have no photos, ask about a typography-led design before work starts.

Will you post it or run ads?

You receive the flyer PNG and caption text by email. You choose where and when to publish. Posting, paid ads, account management and ad spend aren't included. The kit doesn't promise leads, bookings or sales.

How do delivery, revisions and refunds work?

Delivery is within 24 hours after payment and a complete, approved brief. One consolidated revision within the agreed scope is included, requested within seven days and returned within 24 hours of your change list. Cancel before work starts for a full refund. If I can't deliver the agreed work, I'll refund the order. After work starts, email me so we can resolve any issue.

Can you make a full price list too?

Yes. The $59 price-list makeover includes a one-page menu, PDF and PNG, a matching social graphic and one revision. It is a separate service with a separate checkout.
