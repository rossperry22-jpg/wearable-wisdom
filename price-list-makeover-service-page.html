Ask Ross a question ↗
Is this a template or a finished design?

It's a custom design service. I create the layout using your supplied logo, colors, services and approved prices, then deliver finished PDF and PNG files. You don't need to edit a template yourself. The examples show possible design directions.

What do I need to send?

Your exact business name, current service list and prices, logo if you have one, preferred colors, and contact or booking details. Include any “from” prices, durations, add-on rules and other conditions. A photo of an existing menu is fine, as long as you confirm the text and prices.

What if I don't have a logo?

I can style your business name in text as part of the menu. A new standalone logo or full brand identity isn't included in this package.

When will I get my files?

The first design arrives within 24 hours after payment and receipt of complete, approved materials. One consolidated revision is included and returned within 24 hours of your change list. Email before paying if you need a specific same-day deadline.

Can you work with my industry or a longer menu?

Yes—barbers, nail studios, detailers, cleaners and other service businesses can use the package. One page includes up to 15 items; a separately listed add-on or price variant counts as an item. For a longer menu, choose a section or email for a separate quote before paying.

What if I need changes or want to cancel?

One consolidated revision within the agreed scope is included when requested within seven days of the first design. Cancel for a full refund before design work begins. If I can't deliver the agreed work, I'll refund the order. After work starts, email me about any issue so we can resolve it.
